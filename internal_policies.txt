# Internal Company Policies (v2.0)

## 1. Data Retention Policy
- Financial records must be retained for a minimum of 7 years.
- Customer personal data (PII) must be deleted within 30 days of account closure, unless required for legal holds.
- Internal communication logs are archived for 1 year.

## 2. Password and Access Security
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all internal systems.
- Access reviews are conducted quarterly by department heads.

## 3. Remote Work Policy
- Employees must use company-provided VPN for accessing internal tools.
- Public Wi-Fi is prohibited for handling sensitive client data.
- Hard drives on all company laptops must be encrypted.

## 4. AI Usage Policy
- All AI models used for decision-making must be reviewed by the Ethics Committee.
- Customer data must be anonymized before being used for training or external API calls.
- AI-generated reports must be verified by a human before being sent to regulators.

## 5. Travel and Expense
- All business travel requires prior approval from the manager.
- Receipts must be submitted within 15 days of the expense date.
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours.
